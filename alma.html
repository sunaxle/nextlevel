★ Words from the Precinct Chair • Precinct 84 ★

"A Message to My Neighbors in Precinct 84 — We Do More in '84!"

Dear Neighbors, Friends, and Families of Precinct 84,

Welcome to our community hub! As your Democratic Precinct Chair, I live by one guiding motto: "We Do More in '84!"

That means doing more to check in on our elderly neighbors, doing more to defend our neighborhood public schools, doing more to demand safe roads and flood drainage, and doing more to make sure every single registered voter casts their ballot.

Real change doesn't start in Austin or Washington—it starts right here at our kitchen tables and on our front porches. When we stand together as neighbors, our voices cannot be ignored.

Whether you need help checking your voter registration, finding your closest early voting site, or getting a yard sign for your lawn, please call or message me directly on WhatsApp at (956) 878-7362. Let's show all of South Texas what happens when a united community works together!

Meet Your Precinct Chair

Dedicated to Our Families & Neighborhoods

"We do more when we listen to each other, support working families, and make sure our neighborhood is never taken for granted."

Alma Butcher is a dedicated grassroots organizer, community activist, and proud Democratic Precinct Chair for Precinct 84 in Hidalgo County.

As a member of the Hidalgo County Democratic Party County Executive Committee (CEC), Alma works closely with local leaders, teachers, precinct chairs, and neighbors to champion voting rights, public investments, and community empowerment across South Texas.

Alma's 3 Priorities for Precinct 84:

🗳️

1. Maximum Voter Turnout

Ensuring every neighbor in Precinct 84 is registered, educated on candidates, and supported in casting their ballot.

🏘️

2. Neighborhood Action & Infrastructure

Advocating for better drainage, well-lit streets, reliable utilities, and responsive county services.

🤝

3. Block-by-Block Unity

Building strong connections among neighbors through community cleanups, coffee chats, and family resources.
