★ Words from the Precinct Chair • Precinct 35 ★

"A Message to My Neighbors in Precinct 35 & Hidalgo County"

Dear Neighbors and Community Members,

Serving as your Democratic Precinct Chair for Precinct 35 is a deep privilege. Every election begins with everyday conversations right here on our neighborhood streets. My mission is to make sure our community's voice is heard, our voting rights are defended, and our families receive the public investments and resources they deserve.

From supporting great public schools and after-school programs to ensuring reliable neighborhood drainage, safe street lighting, and accessible healthcare for our elders, we move forward when we organize together block by block.

I encourage every resident in Precinct 35 to get involved, check their voter registration, and join us as a neighborhood Block Captain. Please reach out to me directly at (956) 393-7125 whenever you need help or have questions.

🇪🇸 Mensaje en Español para Nuestra Comunidad del Precinto 35: "Nuestra comunidad se fortalece cuando trabajamos unidos por el bienestar de nuestras familias y la educación de nuestros hijos. Como su Presidenta del Precinto 35, estoy a su servicio para ayudarle con información sobre elecciones, ubicaciones para votar y recursos comunitarios. Mándeme un mensaje por WhatsApp o llámeme al (956) 393-7125. ¡Juntos hacemos la diferencia!"

Meet Your Precinct Chair

Dedicated to Our Families & Neighborhoods

"When we organize together as neighbors in Precinct 35, we create lasting opportunities for our children, protect our public services, and build a stronger Rio Grande Valley."

Liza Cardoza is an active community advocate, grassroots organizer, and proud Democratic Precinct Chair for Precinct 35 in Hidalgo County.

Working alongside local grassroots leaders, educators, and neighborhood families, Liza is committed to turning out every vote, educating voters on upcoming ballots, and connecting residents directly to local government resources.

Liza's 3 Community Priorities for Pct. 35:

🗳️

1. Voter Education & Turnout

Ensuring every neighbor understands the ballot, knows their polling locations, and is empowered to vote in every election.

🏘️

2. Neighborhood Quality of Life

Advocating for better infrastructure, paved streets, bright streetlights, and responsive county services.

🤝

3. Grassroots Family Support

Connecting working families, seniors, and students to essential community assistance programs and job training.
