Meet The Slate

Our Next Level Candidates for PSJA

Place 1 Candidate

Dr. Norma Cabrera

Doctor of Education & Instructional Specialist

"Our students deserve world-class early literacy mastery, cutting-edge STEM programs, and real pathways to tuition-free college degrees."

Dr. Norma Cabrera is a lifelong educator and instructional leader with a Doctorate in Educational Leadership. Having served in public school classrooms, curriculum administration, and instructional development, she understands what our students, teachers, and campus staff need to thrive.

As our Place 1 Trustee, Dr. Cabrera will champion competitive teacher retention pay, dual-language bilingual enrichment, expanded pre-K, and high-demand industry certifications across all PSJA high schools.

Place 2 Candidate

Jose L. Bravo, JD

Attorney at Law & Governance Counsel • PSJA Alumnus

"Growing up in PSJA taught me the value of integrity. Public tax dollars belong directly in our classrooms with complete transparency and zero wasteful spending."

Jose L. Bravo, JD is a practicing attorney with extensive expertise in municipal law, governance compliance, and public administration. A proud alumnus of PSJA schools, Jose brings rigorous legal scrutiny to ensure district policies and contracts serve the public interest.

As our Place 2 Trustee, Jose will institute strict vendor procurement ethics, push for live-streamed open meetings, and protect hard-earned taxpayer dollars with independent financial accountability.

Place 3 Candidate

Dr. Vittorio "Vitto" Puente

Doctor of Psychology & Youth Well-Being Advocate

"When we prioritize student mental health, campus security, and family partnerships, our students reach their highest potential."

Dr. Vittorio "Vitto" Puente is a Doctor of Psychology with extensive clinical experience serving children, adolescents, and families across the Rio Grande Valley. He brings vital mental health and safety expertise to the school board.

As our Place 3 Trustee, Dr. Puente is dedicated to fortifying physical school security, stationing certified School Resource Officers on every campus, and expanding counseling and special education resources for all students.
