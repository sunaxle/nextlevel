★ Words from the Precinct Chair • Precinct 146 ★

"A Message to My Neighbors in Precinct 146 & the Rio Grande Valley"

Dear Neighbors and Friends,

Serving as your Democratic Precinct Chair for Precinct 146 is one of the highest honors of my life. Our community is built on hardworking families, devoted educators, local small business owners, and elders who have given everything to build a better future for our children in Pharr, San Juan, and across Hidalgo County.

True democracy doesn't happen in distant capitol halls—it starts right here on our front porches. When we check in on our neighbors, make sure our families are registered to vote, and walk to the polls together at Pharr DRC and our neighborhood voting hubs, we protect our public schools, demand safe and well-lit streets, and secure the vital healthcare and municipal services our community deserves.

As your Precinct Chair, my door and my phone are always open to you. Whether you need help checking your voter registration, finding your nearest early voting location, or organizing your block, I am here to serve. Let's make sure our neighborhood's voice is heard loud and clear at the ballot box.

🇪🇸 Mensaje en Español para Nuestra Comunidad: "Nuestra fuerza comunitaria radica en la unidad de nuestras familias. Como su Presidenta de Precinto 146, estoy comprometida a asegurar que cada voz y cada voto cuente. Nuestro centro de votación principal más cercano es el Pharr DRC (850 W. Dicker Rd). Si tiene preguntas sobre cómo registrarse para votar o necesita transporte a las urnas, llámeme o mándeme un mensaje por WhatsApp al (956) 655-8344. ¡Juntos avanzamos!"
Olga Laura Cardoza

Meet Your Precinct Chair

Dedicated to Our Families & Neighborhoods

"Democracy starts right here on our front porches. When neighbors unite to support working families, great public schools, and community safety, our entire Valley moves forward."

Olga Laura Cardoza is an active grassroots leader, community advocate, and proud Democratic Precinct Chair for Precinct 146 in Hidalgo County.

As a member of the Hidalgo County Democratic Party County Executive Committee (CEC) and an appointee to local civic boards including the City of Pharr Community Development Council, Olga works tirelessly to ensure that working families in our neighborhoods have a powerful, respected voice in local and state government.

Olga's 3 Priorities for Precinct 146:

🗳️

1. Turnout & Voter Protection

Ensuring every eligible neighbor is registered, knows their polling location at Pharr DRC, and has the assistance needed to cast their ballot safely.

🏘️

2. Neighborhood Infrastructure & Safety

Working closely with Pharr, San Juan, and Hidalgo County leaders on street lighting, drainage improvements, and safe routes for our kids.

🤝

3. Block-by-Block Community Care

Connecting seniors, veterans, and young families directly to county resources, social programs, and civic empowerment workshops.
